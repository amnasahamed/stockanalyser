Data Pipeline

2

Download historical price data for all stocks. Supports resume if interrupted.

3

Calculate Indicators

Calculate technical indicators (RSI, MACD, MA, ATR, etc.) for all stocks.

4

Detect Circuits

Identify all UC and LC events using the 1% tolerance rule.

5

Train Models

Train ML models for UC prediction and UC-to-LC duration estimation.

Run Complete Pipeline

Execute all steps in sequence. This may take a while depending on the number of stocks.

Information

UC/LC Detection Rules

  • Upper Circuit (UC): Close price equals High price within 1% tolerance
  • Lower Circuit (LC): Close price equals Low price within 1% tolerance

Technical Indicators Calculated

  • Price changes (1-day, 5-day)
  • Moving averages (5, 20, 50 day)
  • RSI (14-period)
  • MACD (12, 26, 9)
  • Volume ratio (20-day)
  • ATR (14-period)
  • Support/Resistance (20-day)
